This project adapts the Pen's moving-spectrum and blend-mode CSS approach for
Anomaly Hand tactical cards. No source images, masks, textures, or other art
are included. The effect uses card-local motion instead of
background-attachment: fixed because that property is unreliable in iOS Safari.
